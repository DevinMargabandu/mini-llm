--- KV cache vs. no cache, single request (sweeping generation length) ---
new_tokens   no_cache (ms)    cache (ms)   speedup
        64           107.6         215.4     0.50x
       256           415.7         822.8     0.51x
       512          1199.6        1285.0     0.93x
       900          3485.0        2602.5     1.34x

Note: without a cache, every step reprocesses the whole (growing) sequence -- roughly O(n^2) total work over a generation. With a cache, each step only does O(1) work for the new token. For short generations, fixed per-step Python/kernel-dispatch overhead and cache-concatenation bookkeeping outweigh that saving; the crossover in the table above is where the O(n^2) cost catches up.
